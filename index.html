The journey at a glance

From neon nights
to mountain mornings.

Our route begins in Osaka, takes us through Nara and Kyoto, then into the landscapes of Hakone and Mount Fuji before ending in Tokyo. Dates are a working plan for late November to early December 2027.

01 · Days 1–4

Osaka

Street food, Dotonbori, Shinsaibashi, Universal Studios and the aquarium.

02 · Day 3

Nara

A relaxed afternoon with Nara Park, Todai-ji and the old town.

03 · Days 5–7

Kyoto

Traditional streets, shrines, bamboo groves and autumn scenery.

04 · Day 8

Hakone

Lake Ashi, mountain scenery and a ryokan stay.

05 · Day 9

Kawaguchiko

Lake views, Oishi Park and the chance to see Mount Fuji.

06 · Days 10–13

Tokyo

Shibuya, Asakusa, Harajuku, Ginza and the journey home.
